1. Privacy Policy Overview

Welcome to GlobalSwap, developed and operated by MD MAHIN ISLAM ("we", "our", or "developer"). GlobalSwap provides users with digital currency and exchange tracking, rate calculation, and transaction logging services. Your privacy and data integrity are essential to us. This Privacy Policy details how we handle user information when you use GlobalSwap.

We strictly believe in user data protection. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal contact or transaction records to third-party commercial marketing brokers.

2. Information We Collect and Process

To facilitate exchange inquiries and transaction workflows, the app handles the following data:

  • User Profile Data: Basic contact details (such as your email address, username, or phone/WhatsApp number) when creating an account or submitting an exchange request.
  • Order & Transaction Records: User-submitted order logs, including currency pairs, sending/receiving payment identifiers (e.g., wallet addresses, transaction IDs, account numbers), and timestamp data necessary to process and verify orders.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Anonymized crash reports, device model, and OS version to maintain application stability, prevent security vulnerabilities, and resolve technical bugs.
Security Notice: GlobalSwap does not store private cryptocurrency wallet seed phrases, private cryptographic keys, or banking passwords.

3. Data Safety, Security & Retention

In compliance with Google Play's Financial Services and Data Safety guidelines:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between GlobalSwap and cloud databases (e.g., Supabase / REST endpoints) is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: Transaction logs are retained only as required to resolve order disputes, verify completed transfers, and comply with standard operational accounting. Users may request complete deletion of their account records by emailing our support address.
  • Restricted Third-Party Access: We do not grant access to your transactional data to unauthorized external entities.

4. Terms of Use & Financial Risk Disclaimer

By registering or initiating an exchange request on GlobalSwap, you acknowledge and agree to the following conditions:

  1. Exchange Rates & Order Processing: Exchange conversion rates are subject to real-time market fluctuations. Orders are processed according to the finalized transaction details submitted by the user.
  2. Accuracy of User Inputs: You are solely responsible for verifying the accuracy of recipient wallet addresses, bank account numbers, or mobile financial accounts before submitting a transaction. The developer cannot reverse transfers sent to erroneous user-provided destinations.
  3. Compliance & Lawful Use: You agree not to utilize GlobalSwap for unlawful activities, fraud, or unauthorized financial transactions. Accounts suspected of malicious activity will be terminated immediately.
Risk Warning: Digital currencies and cryptocurrencies involve inherent price volatility and network risks. Users should exercise due diligence when transacting digital assets.
